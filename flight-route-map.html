Plan a multi-stop trip across time zones

Long trips with connections are hard to reason about: each leg crosses time zones, so the clock at your layover and final destination is nothing like the one you left. This map plots the great-circle route between the world's major airports and works out the real local time you arrive and leave at every stop, layovers included.

Need just one leg? Use the flight time calculator. Wondering how you'll feel on arrival? Try the jet lag calculator. It works from the durations you enter — no live flight data.

Frequently asked questions

How does the flight route map work?+

Add airports in order, set the first departure and each flight's duration and layovers. The map draws the route and the itinerary shows local arrival and departure times at every stop.

Can I add layovers or multiple stops?+

Yes — add as many airports as your trip has, with a layover at each intermediate stop, and the times carry forward across every time zone.

Does it account for time zones?+

Yes — each airport uses its IANA time zone, so local times and the arrival day are correct, with daylight saving handled automatically.

Does it use real flight schedules?+

No — it works from the durations and layovers you enter, with no live flight data. Coordinates cover the world's major airports.

Is it free?+

Yes — free, no signup, runs in your browser.

How to convert a time by hand

Move through UTC: subtract the first zone's offset from the time, then add the second zone's offset.

Example: 2:00 PM AEST to SGT. AEST is UTC+10, so 14:00 minus 10 hours is 04:00 UTC. SGT is UTC+8, so 04:00 plus 8 hours is 12:00 PM.

Example: 9:00 PM PST to JST. PST is UTC−8, so 21:00 plus 8 hours is 05:00 UTC the next day. JST is UTC+9, so the result is 2:00 PM the next day.

The offset depends on the date. New York is UTC−5 in January and UTC−4 in July, which is why this converter asks for a date as well as a time.

Common abbreviations

In this converter, EST, CST, MST, PST, CET and AEST follow the real clock of their region on the date you choose. EST on a July date therefore uses UTC−4, which is strictly EDT, because that is what most people mean when they write EST all year.

Abbreviations that mean more than one zone

  • CST: US Central (UTC−6), China (UTC+8) or Cuba (UTC−5). This converter uses US Central. For China, choose Shanghai.
  • IST: India (UTC+5:30), Israel (UTC+2) or Irish Summer Time (UTC+1). This converter uses India. For the others, choose Jerusalem or Dublin if listed.
  • BST: British Summer Time (UTC+1) or Bangladesh (UTC+6). This converter uses a fixed UTC+1. For the UK all year, choose London.
  • GST: Gulf (UTC+4) or South Georgia (UTC−2). This converter uses Gulf.

When a message only gives an abbreviation, ask which zone is meant, or choose the city instead.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between EST and EDT?+

EST is UTC−5 and EDT is UTC−4. New York uses EDT from March to November and EST the rest of the year. Many people write EST all year, so this converter follows the clock in New York on your chosen date.

Does the converter handle daylight saving time?+

Yes. It uses the rules in the IANA Time Zone Database for the date you pick, so a conversion for January and one for July can give different results.

Why does the result say next day or previous day?+

The two places can be on different calendar dates at the same moment. The label shows the date in the second place compared with the first.

Can I share a conversion?+

Yes. The page address updates as you change the zones, time and date, so you can copy the link and the other person sees the same conversion.

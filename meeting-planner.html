How to plan a meeting across time zones

Add every participant's city. Each city gets a 24-hour row with working hours (9:00 to 18:00) shaded and night hours dimmed. Pick a date, because the overlap can change when a country changes its clocks. Columns where everyone is working turn green.

When no hour suits everyone, the planner suggests the closest one, with the most people in working hours and the fewest asleep. Click any column to see each person's local time and add it to a calendar.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the best time to meet across time zones?+

Add each participant's city. Columns where everyone is in working hours turn green. If there are none, the planner suggests the closest hour.

What if there is no time that works for everyone?+

The planner picks the hour with the most people in working hours and the fewest in the middle of the night. Rotating who takes the inconvenient slot keeps it fair.

Does the planner account for daylight saving time?+

Yes. It uses the rules for the date you pick, so a meeting planned for next month reflects any clock change before then.

Can I share the plan?+

Yes. Share this plan copies a link with your cities. Anyone who opens it can add them to their own planner.

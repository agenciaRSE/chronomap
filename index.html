Interactive World Clock Map

ChronoMap is a free interactive world clock and time zone map. See the current local time anywhere on a 2D world map or a 3D globe, hover any country to read its live time, pin and compare multiple cities side by side, and travel forwards or backwards through time.

Every location shows its real local time using its IANA time zone, so daylight saving time is always handled correctly. Click the map or search for a city to add it, and the globe smoothly rotates to centre on your selection.

Explore ChronoMap

  • Interactive time zone map — the current time in every UTC offset, from UTC−12 to UTC+14, with live clocks for major cities.
  • Time zone converter — convert any time between zones and cities.
  • Jet lag recovery calculator — how many days it takes to recover after crossing time zones, east or west.
  • Flight arrival time calculator — what time you land in local time, or the real duration of a flight.
  • Embeddable clock widget — a free live clock for any city that you can embed on your website.

  • Discord timestamp generator — turn any date and time into a code that renders in each user's own time zone.

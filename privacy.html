Privacy

ChronoMap has no accounts and sets no cookies of its own. The cities you pin stay in your browser.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who is responsible

Agenciarse (Agencia RSE) runs this site. For any privacy request, email dev@agenciarse.com.

What stays on your device

Pinned cities and meeting planner rows are saved in your browser's localStorage and are not sent to us. You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

If you create a share link, the selected cities, time and map view are encoded in that link. Anyone you send it to can read them.

What is processed when you visit

  • Hosting (Vercel): serving a page requires technical data such as your IP address and browser details.
  • Audience and speed measurement (Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights): cookieless, used to see which pages are visited and how fast they load. It does not run on the embeddable clock.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts): your browser downloads fonts from Google, which receives your IP address.

We use this data to run, secure and improve the site. We do not sell it and we do not show ads.

Other services

The news bar headlines come from The Commons and reach your browser through our own server. If you install the Windows app, the Microsoft Store handles that download under its own terms.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, and you can object to its processing, by emailing the address above. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

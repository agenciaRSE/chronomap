How the estimate works

Jet lag happens when your body clock is still on home time after you arrive. According to the CDC Yellow Book, people adapt at an average of about 1 hour per day after flying east and 1.5 hours per day after flying west. This calculator divides the time difference by those rates.

East is harder because most people's internal clock runs slightly longer than 24 hours, so going to bed later comes more easily than going to bed earlier.

If the gap is more than 12 hours, it is counted the shorter way round the clock. Los Angeles to Sydney is treated as about 5 or 6 hours west, not 18 or 19 hours east. Jet lag is usually defined as starting from two time zones, and the number of zones is only one factor: time cues at the destination, individual genetics and medication also matter.

What the evidence supports

  • Before you fly: moving your sleep 1 hour per day for 2 or 3 days can shorten the adjustment. Go earlier when flying east and later when flying west.
  • On the plane: stay hydrated and limit alcohol and caffeine, which can disturb sleep.
  • On arrival: stay awake during the local day. Naps of 20 to 30 minutes can help, but longer ones can make the night harder.
  • Light: bright morning light moves your clock earlier and evening light moves it later. The best timing depends on how many zones you crossed, which this calculator does not model.
  • Melatonin: timing matters, and the wrong time can make things worse. The CDC notes that 0.5 to 1 mg is often enough, advises against more than 5 mg, and warns that doses on labels may be inaccurate. Ask a doctor first, especially if you take other medication.
  • Short trips: if you are only away for a short time, keeping your home sleep hours may be better than adapting.

Source: CDC Yellow Book 2026, Jet Lag Disorder. ChronoMap is not a medical service and this page is not medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

How long does jet lag last?+

On average, about one day per time zone after flying east and about two days for every three zones after flying west, based on the rates in the CDC Yellow Book. It varies a lot between people.

Is east or west jet lag worse?+

East is usually harder. It requires moving your body clock earlier, and most people find that more difficult than moving it later.

How many time zones cause jet lag?+

Jet lag disorder is defined as involving travel across at least two time zones. A one-hour change is unlikely to cause more than a slightly off night.

How is this different from an app with a personal plan?+

This calculator gives averages and a simple sleep schedule. Dedicated apps use your flight times to suggest when to seek or avoid light. The CDC suggests considering such calculators for personal timing.

Does it need my flight details?+

No. It only compares the time zones of the two cities and uses your usual bedtime. Nothing is sent to us.
